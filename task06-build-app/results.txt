Задание 6. Сборка приложения и архива

Команды выполнены в task06-build-app.

$ mkdir myapp
$ printf "%s\n" "print('hello from myapp')" > myapp/main.py
$ printf '%s\n' '1.0.0' > myapp/VERSION

$ COPYFILE_DISABLE=1 tar -czf myapp-1.0.0.tar.gz -C myapp .

$ stat -f '%N %z bytes' myapp-1.0.0.tar.gz
myapp-1.0.0.tar.gz 358 bytes

$ tar -tzf myapp-1.0.0.tar.gz
./
./VERSION
./main.py

$ tar -xOzf myapp-1.0.0.tar.gz ./main.py
print('hello from myapp')

$ tar -xOzf myapp-1.0.0.tar.gz ./VERSION
1.0.0

$ python3 myapp/main.py
hello from myapp
